## 1. Probe Charter

### Operational purpose

Этот bounded `LIMITED PROBE` не отвечает на всю исходную исследовательскую территорию. Его единственная проектная цель — **route discrimination**:

> установить, достаточно ли прямого и устойчивого evidence в наиболее диагностических источниках и существующем research coverage, чтобы оправдать более широкий Research Project, либо вопрос должен быть сужен, разделён, переформулирован или закрыт.

Probe не предполагает заранее, что generosity, trust in divine care/provision и stewardship/entrustedness образуют одну doctrine или одну устойчивую biblical structure.

### Success condition

Probe успешен не тогда, когда hypothesis подтверждена, а когда evidence позволяет обоснованно выбрать следующий route.

Допустимы:

- strong viability;
- partial viability;
- different structures;
- weak viability / negative result.

Ни один из этих outcomes автоматически не запускает Full Project.

## 2. Research Question and Scope

### Route-discrimination question

> Есть ли в существующем scholarly research и в трёх наиболее диагностических biblical corpora достаточно прямого evidence, чтобы рассматривать связь generosity/giving, trust in divine care/provision и stewardship/entrustedness как жизнеспособную основу более широкого Research Project?

### Scope IN

1. Existing Research Coverage Review по relation:
   - generosity / giving;
   - trust in divine care or provision;
   - stewardship / entrustedness.
2. Отдельная проверка coverage для:
   - Мф. 6:19–34;
   - Лк. 12:13–34;
   - 2 Кор. 8–9.
3. Если Coverage Gate возвращает `TEXTUAL PROBE STILL REQUIRED`, conditional textual work только по этим трём corpora.
4. Проверка как supporting, так и challenging/negative evidence.

### Scope OUT

- полный OT/NT corpus;
- Acts, 1 Tim 6, 1 Pet 4 и другие potential Full Territory candidates;
- time, attention, opportunities, abilities/talents как вывод из material-resource evidence;
- comprehensive theology of wealth, work or providence;
- practical generosity manual;
- «Щедрость и власть»;
- downstream publication architecture;
- Full Project architecture.

### Required distinctions

- generosity ≠ stewardship;
- provision ≠ transactional reward;
- trust не предполагается универсальным мотивом giving;
- fear of scarcity остаётся hypothesis;
- entrustedness требует textual warrant;
- material findings не переносятся автоматически на nonmaterial resources.

### Epistemic rules

1. Existing research и biblical text являются разными evidence layers; внешний research не заменяется Formation scan.
2. Pastoral/popular reception может показывать application/reception, но не считается эквивалентом scholarly evidence.
3. Source, observation, interpretation, hypothesis, challenge evidence и synthesis должны оставаться различимыми.
4. Negative или mixed evidence сохраняется и может изменить route.
5. Cross-corpus synthesis допускается только после отдельного рассмотрения каждого из трёх corpora.
6. Сила judgment должна быть пропорциональна evidence; отсутствие scholarly coverage не является evidence истинности или ложности biblical hypothesis.
7. Никакой stage не расширяет corpus самостоятельно.

## 8. Coverage Gate

Coverage Review must return exactly one primary judgment. Secondary notes may be recorded but may not blur the gate.

### A. `EXISTING RESEARCH PACKAGE SUFFICIENT`

Use only when a credible, traceable scholarly package already addresses the central relation with enough direct engagement across the diagnostic corpora to make new primary textual Probe disproportionate for route discrimination.

**Action:** STOP before textual work → Project Lead handoff → Director / Owner route decision.

### B. `LIMITED REVIEW SUFFICIENT`

Use when existing scholarship substantially covers the question, but a bounded additional literature synthesis/review can resolve the remaining route uncertainty more proportionately than primary textual Probe.

**Action:** STOP before textual work → return to Director / Owner for a Limited Review route decision.

### C. `TEXTUAL PROBE STILL REQUIRED`

Use when external research does not resolve central textual viability, the route-discrimination question remains materially intact, and direct examination of the three bounded corpora remains proportionate.

**Action:** Project Lead may prepare transition to conditional textual stages only under the execution authority established after the Installation gate. No corpus expansion.

### D. `RESEARCH QUESTION REQUIRES REFORMULATION`

Use when external evidence materially changes terminology, relation structure, scope or assumptions such that executing the installed textual question would test the wrong or materially distorted question.

**Action:** STOP → Director / Owner before textual work.

### Gate safeguard

A thin or inconclusive literature base does not automatically mean `TEXTUAL PROBE STILL REQUIRED`; the judgment must explain why direct textual work is the proportionate next method rather than further bounded review or reformulation.

## 9. Textual Probe Boundary

If activated, textual work is limited to:

- **Matthew 6:19–34**;
- **Luke 12:13–34**;
- **2 Corinthians 8–9**.

Matthew/Luke may be compared as related Synoptic material, but observations must preserve passage-level distinctions. 2 Corinthians must first be examined on its own terms before cross-corpus comparison.

No other biblical text becomes part of the active Probe merely because it appears in Formation as potential, supporting or challenge material. A material need to add corpus triggers `RETURN FOR CLARIFICATION` / route reconsideration rather than silent expansion.

## 10. Epistemic Outcomes

### Strong viability

Direct evidence across the bounded corpora supports a sufficiently coherent relation to make a broader project a proportionate candidate. This is not Full Project authorization.

### Partial viability

Some relations are supported, but one or more elements are weak, indirect or corpus-specific. Route should narrow or reframe.

### Different structures

Synoptic and Pauline material produce materially different theological/semantic structures that should not be collapsed. Route may split the territory or reformulate the question.

### Weak viability / negative result

The proposed central relation lacks enough direct support in the diagnostic corpora to justify the broader project in its current form. Close, defer or materially redesign remain legitimate outcomes.

## 13. Stop and Close Conditions

### Immediate STOP / return before textual work

- `EXISTING RESEARCH PACKAGE SUFFICIENT`;
- `LIMITED REVIEW SUFFICIENT`;
- `RESEARCH QUESTION REQUIRES REFORMULATION`;
- external evidence materially changes the installed question;
- required corpus, authority, Protocol or Output Contract becomes conflicting;
- material scope expansion is needed;
- independence requirement cannot be satisfied.

### PARTIAL STOP

Only when an explicit authority decision allows unaffected work to continue while a bounded issue is escalated.

### Probe Close

The Probe closes when either:

1. Coverage Gate routes the work away from textual Probe; or
2. conditional textual stages are completed and a bounded viability judgment is accepted; or
3. the Probe is honestly BLOCKED/DEFERRED by authority or evidence conditions.

At close, Project Lead returns outputs, unresolved questions and route evidence to Director for research-route re-evaluation, with Owner gate where required.
