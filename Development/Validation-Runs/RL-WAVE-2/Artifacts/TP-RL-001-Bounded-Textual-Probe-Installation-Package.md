## 1. Installed purpose and exact Probe question

### Purpose

Проверить только остаточную primary-text uncertainty, принятую после LR-RL-001, не расширяя тему в систематическое библейское богословие stewardship и не предпосылая положительный ответ.

### Installed Probe question

> Дают ли Мф. 6:19–34 и Лк. 12:13–34, рассматриваемые каждый в собственных литературных границах, локальное текстовое основание понимать материальные блага как доверенное Богом для ответственного распоряжения — как отличимое звено между giving и trust/provision, — либо такая entrustedness/stewardship-модель требует более широкого богословского контекста?

### Operational discriminator

> Какой именно local textual warrant — lexical, syntactic, argumentative, narrative/discourse или иной непосредственно наблюдаемый в locus — поддерживает либо не поддерживает это третье звено?

Probe не должен доказывать stewardship, не должен защищать ранее предложенную трёхчастную модель и допускает отрицательный, частичный или асимметричный результат.

7. **Entrustedness/stewardship discrimination** — distinguish:
   - responsibility/accountability,
   - divine ownership/source of goods,
   - delegated management/entrustedness,
   - trust/provision,
   and state which relations are locally warranted.
8. **Exactly one primary Probe outcome:**
   - `LOCAL WARRANT ESTABLISHED IN BOTH LOCI`
   - `LOCAL WARRANT ESTABLISHED IN ONE LOCUS ONLY`
   - `LOCAL WARRANT PARTIAL / INDETERMINATE`
   - `LOCAL WARRANT NOT ESTABLISHED WITHIN THE BOUNDED LOCI`
   - `QUESTION REQUIRES MATERIAL CORPUS REFORMULATION`
9. **Rationale**, including why the other four outcomes were not selected.
10. **Uncertainty / confidence / limitations**.
11. **Decision needed / next route**, without activating it.
12. **Handoff:** Researcher → Research Project Lead.

### Outcome semantics

**LOCAL WARRANT ESTABLISHED IN BOTH LOCI**  
Use only if each locus independently supplies sufficient local textual basis for the proposed entrustedness/stewardship link.

**LOCAL WARRANT ESTABLISHED IN ONE LOCUS ONLY**  
Use when one locus meets that threshold and the other does not.

**LOCAL WARRANT PARTIAL / INDETERMINATE**  
Use when local evidence is genuinely mixed, underdetermined, or insufficiently discriminating without yet proving corpus reformulation necessary.

**LOCAL WARRANT NOT ESTABLISHED WITHIN THE BOUNDED LOCI**  
Use when the bounded loci do not supply sufficient local basis for the stronger entrustedness/stewardship model. This is not a universal theological negation.

**QUESTION REQUIRES MATERIAL CORPUS REFORMULATION**  
Use when the question cannot responsibly be resolved within the installed loci because the relevant warrant necessarily depends on materially broader corpus/context. Do not perform that expansion inside this Probe.

## 8. Safeguards against importing the target concept

Researcher must:

- begin from observations, not from the desired stewardship label;
- record evidence that supports simpler alternatives such as allegiance, dependence, generosity, responsibility or anti-anxiety without delegated-management implications;
- distinguish God as provider/source from God as owner/entruster unless the locus warrants the latter;
- distinguish moral responsibility from entrusted administration;
- avoid using 2 Cor 8–9 to fill gaps in the Synoptic loci;
- avoid treating a scholarly formulation from LR-RL-001 as primary-text evidence;
- explicitly note when a proposed relation depends on inferential stacking rather than a direct local textual link.
