## 1. Installed purpose and research question

### Purpose

Установить, что принятый Stage 1 scholarly set уже позволяет утверждать — и чего он не позволяет утверждать — о связи giving/generosity, trust in divine care/provision и stewardship/entrustedness внутри трёх diagnostic corpora, не выполняя новую экзегезу и не возобновляя широкий поиск литературы.

Limited Review является route-discrimination method. Он не должен подтверждать исходную тройную relation как условие успеха.

### Installed question

> Как прочитанные и принятые в Stage 1 академические исследования описывают связи между giving/generosity, trust in divine care/provision и stewardship/entrustedness в Мф. 6:19–34, Лк. 12:13–34 и 2 Кор. 8–9; какие из этих связей имеют passage-specific warrant, какие возникают как более широкий theological synthesis, и остаётся ли после их сопоставления точно определимая textual uncertainty, которую existing research не разрешает?

## 6. Output Contract — LR-RL-001-OC1-v0.1

The completed Review must return one traceable output containing:

1. **Corpus-by-corpus comparison table:** source/locus → scholarly claim → relation type → warrant → limitation/counter-position → confidence.
2. **Stewardship/entrustedness judgment:** for each relevant claim classify as passage-specific, broader synthesis, adjacent category, or unsupported/not established at the inspected locus.
3. **Cross-corpus comparison:** preserve different structures and avoid forced unification.
4. **Source-boundary statement:** identify exactly which accepted sources/loci were used and at what reading depth.
5. **Uncertainty and confidence:** distinguish evidence gap, terminology mismatch and genuine unresolved question.
6. **Exactly one primary route judgment:**
   - `EXISTING KNOWLEDGE SUFFICIENT — CLOSE / TRANSFER`
   - `RESEARCH QUESTION NARROWED / REFORMULATION REQUIRED`
   - `SPECIFIC TEXTUAL UNCERTAINTY REMAINS`
   - `REVIEW BLOCKED BY MATERIAL SOURCE GAP`
7. **Rationale proportional to evidence**, including why the other route outcomes were not selected.
8. **Decision needed / next route**, without activating it.
9. **Handoff:** Researcher → Research Project Lead.

### Outcome semantics

**EXISTING KNOWLEDGE SUFFICIENT — CLOSE / TRANSFER**  
Use when the accepted literature resolves the route question sufficiently that new primary research is disproportionate. This does not mean scholarly consensus.

**RESEARCH QUESTION NARROWED / REFORMULATION REQUIRED**  
Use when comparison materially changes the relation, terminology or scope such that the current research question should not continue unchanged.

**SPECIFIC TEXTUAL UNCERTAINTY REMAINS**  
Use only when the Review can name a bounded primary-text question and show why the accepted literature does not resolve it. Action is STOP and route handoff; Textual Probe remains unauthorized.

**REVIEW BLOCKED BY MATERIAL SOURCE GAP**  
Use when a concrete missing scholarly resource prevents responsible route judgment. The output must name the gap, the resource/access needed if identifiable, and why current evidence cannot substitute for it.

## 7. Uncertainty, limitations and confidence

The Review must carry forward at minimum:

- direct coverage of a pair does not establish the full three-way relation;
- thesis/practical-theology studies do not establish disciplinary representativeness;
- “entrustedness not established” must not become “entrustedness absent”;
- broader canonical warrant used by scholars does not expand the Review's diagnostic corpus;
- different organising categories do not automatically constitute disagreement;
- source availability and bibliographic identification do not imply endorsement of an author's reconstruction;
- confidence begins from the accepted Stage 1 level of MODERATE and may change only for explicitly evidenced reasons.

## 8. Stop conditions

- a material source gap prevents responsible judgment;

- corpus expansion becomes necessary;
- the question materially changes beyond the installed route;
- Protocol/Output Contract/authority conflict emerges;
- execution would require claiming systematic completeness or scholarly consensus not supported by the evidence.

Unaffected work may continue only if the active authority explicitly permits a PARTIAL STOP. This Installation does not itself grant such expansion authority.
