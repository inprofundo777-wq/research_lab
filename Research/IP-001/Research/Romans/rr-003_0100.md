# 1. Stage Purpose

Stage 3 отвечает на вопрос:

> какие provisional research groups действительно возникают из наблюдений Romans после Primary Observation и Repetition Mapping?

Это не окончательная богословская taxonomy.

Группа допускается только тогда, когда она:

1. возникает из фактического evidence;
2. сохраняет различия attribution;
3. не требует превращать relation в action;
4. не скрывает существенную неоднородность passages;
5. позволяет восстановить путь от группы к локальному тексту.

Рабочая последовательность:

> Stage 1 local evidence  
> → Stage 2 repetition  
> → Stage 3 provisional research group.

---

# 2. Classification Boundary

Stage 2 показал, что evidence Romans неоднородно.

В нём присутствуют:

- explicit continuing actions;
- mediated actions;
- continuing functions;
- salvific mediation;
- present relations;
- strong singular actions;
- contextual relations;
- unresolved BORDERLINE evidence.

Поэтому Stage 3 не использует единственную категорию:

> «действия Христа».

Вместо этого каждая группа получает **evidence type**, чтобы не сделать classification сильнее исходного текста.

# 3. Research Group RG-R01
## Salvific Mediation through Christ

### Core Evidence

- Рим. 1:5
- Рим. 5:1–2
- Рим. 5:9–11
- Рим. 5:15–21

### Repeated Form

Romans repeatedly uses formulations such as:

> «через Иисуса Христа»

> «посредством Которого»

> «посредством единого Иисуса Христа»

> «Им»

> «жизнью Его».

Through Christ believers receive or are related to:

- grace;
- apostolic commission;
- access to grace;
- reconciliation;
- salvation;
- life.

### Evidence Type

**Mediated salvific agency / mediation**

### Provisional Classification

> **Христос является посредником спасительных благ и спасительного действия.**

### Confidence

**HIGH — as repeated mediation**

### Important Limitation

Это ещё не означает:

> каждое спасительное благо в этих passages является новым продолжающимся действием воскресшего Христа.

Некоторые formulations могут описывать:

- continuing efficacy of Christ's historical work;
- divine action through Christ;
- activity of the living Christ.

Поэтому группа классифицирует прежде всего:

> **mediation**

а не автоматически:

> **direct continuing action**.

### Verification Need

Особенно:

- Рим. 5:9–10 — «спасемся Им / жизнью Его»;
- temporal/action force спасительной mediation.

# 4. Research Group RG-R02
## Risen Christ's Lordship and Rule

### Core Evidence

- Рим. 10:9–12
- Рим. 14:8–9
- Рим. 15:12

### Supporting Context

- Рим. 12:11
- Рим. 14:18
- Рим. 16:18

### Explicit Anchor

Рим. 14:9:

> «Христос для того и умер, и воскрес, и ожил, чтобы владычествовать и над мертвыми и над живыми».

### Evidence Type

**Continuing function / rule**

### Provisional Classification

> **Воскресший Христос осуществляет господство / владычество.**

### Observed Sphere

His lordship is related to:

- living;
- dead;
- believers who belong to Him;
- those who call upon Him;
- nations.

### Confidence

**HIGH**

Рим. 14:9 непосредственно связывает:

> resurrection  
> → continuing lordship.

Поэтому existence группы не зависит полностью от менее ясных supporting passages.

### Verification Need

Рим. 15:12 требует intertextual confirmation точной силы:

> «восстанет владеть народами».

Но даже если этот passage будет ослаблен, RG-R02 сохраняется на основании Romans 14.

---

# 5. Research Group RG-R03
## Christ's Continuing Agency in Mission

### Core Evidence

- Рим. 15:18
- Рим. 1:5

### Supporting Context

- Рим. 15:16
- Рим. 15:19

### Explicit Anchor

Рим. 15:18:

> «чего не совершил Христос через меня, в покорении язычников вере, словом и делом».

### Evidence Type

**Mediated continuing action**

### Provisional Classification

> **Воскресший Христос действует через человеческого служителя в миссии.**

### Agency Structure

The passage preserves three distinguishable levels:

> **Christ — acting subject**

→

> **Paul — human mediator / servant**

→

> **Gentiles — recipients / sphere of result**

The surrounding context additionally contains:

> **Spirit — power associated with signs, wonders and mission.**

### Observed Result

> «покорение язычников вере».

### Confidence

**HIGH for the explicit action in Рим. 15:18**

**MODERATE for repetition across Romans**

Рим. 15:18 alone establishes the action very strongly.

Рим. 1:5 provides compatible earlier evidence:

> apostolic grace and commission received through Christ.

But the precise relation between 1:5 and 15:18 should not yet be overstated.

### Classification Decision

Keep this group separate from RG-R01.

Reason:

RG-R01 concerns:

> salvific benefits mediated through Christ.

RG-R03 concerns:

> Christ Himself explicitly acting through a human mediator.

Collapsing them would erase an important attribution distinction.

# 6. Research Group RG-R04
## Christ's Present Heavenly Intercession

### Core Evidence

- Рим. 8:34

### Evidence

> «Христос Иисус умер, но и воскрес: Он и одесную Бога, Он и ходатайствует за нас».

### Evidence Type

**Direct continuing action**

### Provisional Classification

> **Воскресший Христос ходатайствует за верующих.**

### Contextual Position

The passage explicitly establishes:

- Christ died;
- Christ rose;
- Christ is at God's right hand;
- Christ intercedes.

Thus the action is unambiguously post-resurrection.

### Confidence

**VERY HIGH**

### Repetition Status

**Strong singular evidence**

No second equally explicit Christ-intercession passage was identified within Romans.

### Classification Decision

Retain as an independent research group despite lack of repetition.

Reason:

> repetition is evidence for stability, not a prerequisite for preserving a clearly attested action.

Suppressing a strong singular action merely because it occurs once would distort the corpus.

---

# 7. Research Group RG-R05
## Christ in Eschatological Judgment

### Core Evidence

- Рим. 2:16

### Supporting / Verification Evidence

- Рим. 14:10
- relation to Рим. 14:9

### Explicit Evidence

Рим. 2:16:

> «Бог будет судить тайные дела человеков через Иисуса Христа».

### Evidence Type

**Mediated future action / judicial function**

### Provisional Classification

> **Бог осуществит суд через Иисуса Христа.**

This wording is deliberately narrower than:

> «Христос судит человечество».

The latter may ultimately be justified, but Рим. 2:16 itself preserves:

- God as explicit subject;
- Christ as mediation of judgment.

### Confidence

**HIGH for mediation in judgment**

### Verification Need

Рим. 14:10 requires textual verification:

> «суд Христов» / «суд Божий».

If «суд Христов» is textually supported for the relevant research base, it may strengthen classification toward:

> **Christ's judicial role/action.**

Until then, RG-R05 retains the more precise formulation:

> **eschatological judgment through Christ.**

# 8. Research Group RG-R06
## Risen Christ's Life toward God

### Core Evidence

- Рим. 6:9–10

### Evidence

> «Христос, воскреснув из мертвых, уже не умирает»

and:

> «а что живет, то живет для Бога».

### Evidence Type

**Continuing action/state**

### Provisional Classification

> **Воскресший Христос живёт для Бога.**

### Confidence

**VERY HIGH**

### Repetition Status

**Strong singular evidence**

### Classification Limitation

Do not yet absorb this into:

- lordship;
- heavenly ministry;
- priesthood;
- obedience;
- intercession.

Romans itself gives the narrower statement:

> **He lives for God.**

That specificity should remain visible.

# 9. Research Group RG-R07
## Christ's Present Presence / Relation in Believers

### Core Evidence

- Рим. 8:9–10

### Supporting Evidence

- Рим. 10:12
- Рим. 12:5
- Рим. 14:8
- repeated «во Христе / в Господе» formulations

### Explicit Anchor

> «А если Христос в вас...»

### Evidence Type

**Presence / continuing relation**

### Provisional Classification

> **Воскресший Христос находится в продолжающемся отношении присутствия к верующим.**

### Confidence

**HIGH for present relation**

### Action Status

**UNRESOLVED AS ACTION**

Stage 3 deliberately does not rewrite:

> «Христос в вас»

as:

> «Христос входит в верующих»

or:

> «Христос вселяется».

Those verbs are not supplied by the local text.

### Classification Decision

Retain the group because it answers an important secondary research question:

> how is the risen Christ presently related to believers?

But label it explicitly as:

> **relation/presence evidence**

rather than direct action.

# 10. Research Group RG-R08
## Christ's Present Provision toward Those Who Call on Him

### Core Evidence

- Рим. 10:9–13

### Explicit Evidence

> «один Господь у всех, богатый для всех, призывающих Его».

### Evidence Type

**Continuing disposition / provision / lordship relation**

### Provisional Classification

> **Воскресший Господь находится в действующем благотворном отношении ко всем, кто призывает Его.**

### Confidence

**MODERATE–HIGH**

The Christ referent of «Господь» is strongly supported by the immediate sequence beginning with:

> «исповедывать Иисуса Господом».

However exact classification of:

> «богатый для всех»

as action, provision, disposition or status should remain provisional.

### Relation to RG-R02

This evidence participates in Christ's lordship.

But it should not disappear entirely inside RG-R02 because it adds a distinct relational feature:

> the Lord is available / abundant toward those who invoke Him.

### Provisional Decision

Keep as a **subgroup candidate**, not yet necessarily a permanent independent group.

# 11. Unresolved Candidate Group

### Evidence

Рим. 11:26:

> «придет от Сиона Избавитель, и отвратит нечестие от Иакова».

### Potential Actions

If the Deliverer is Christ:

1. **He comes;**
2. **He turns ungodliness away from Jacob.**

### Current Status

**BORDERLINE — not admitted into established Research Groups**

### Reason

The action is explicit.

The acting subject is explicit:

> «Избавитель».

But identification:

> **Deliverer = Jesus Christ**

requires intertextual and contextual verification.

### Potential Classification

If verified:

> **Christ's future deliverance / action toward Israel**

and possibly:

> **Christ's future coming.**

This should not yet be merged into RG-R05 or RG-R02.

# 15. Cross-Group Attribution Structure

The provisional groups reveal several different agency structures.

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **Christ acts directly** | Christ intercedes — 8:34 |
| **Christ exercises continuing function** | Christ rules — 14:9 |
| **Christ acts through a human** | Christ acts through Paul — 15:18 |
| **God acts through Christ** | God judges through Christ — 2:16 |
| **salvific benefit comes through Christ** | access / reconciliation / life — Romans 5 |
| **Christ is presently related to believers** | Christ in you — 8:10 |
| **human acts toward Christ** | calls upon / serves / belongs to Lord |
| **God acts distinctly from Christ** | justifies, calls, raises, grafts, crushes Satan |
| **Spirit acts distinctly** | leads, testifies, helps, intercedes |

This distinction is one of the strongest results of the normalized Romans re-run.

# 17. Provisional Answer Emerging from Romans

Romans does not portray the risen Christ merely as the historical basis of salvation.

At minimum, the corpus presents Him after resurrection as One who:

> **lives for God;**

> **is Lord and exercises rule over the living and the dead;**

> **intercedes for believers at God's right hand;**

> **acts through apostolic ministry;**

> **stands in a continuing relation of presence to believers;**

> **mediates salvific benefits;**

> **participates as mediator in God's future judgment.**

Additional possible actions remain unresolved pending verification.

This is a **provisional corpus-level classification**, not yet the final answer of Romans and not the answer of IP-001 as a whole.

---

# 18. Important Negative Finding

The classification also demonstrates what Romans does **not** permit the research to do.

It does not permit the formula:

> everything God does in salvation = Christ does.

Romans repeatedly distinguishes:

- God's agency;
- Christ's agency;
- Spirit's agency;
- human agency.

It also distinguishes:

- direct action;
- mediated action;
- role;
- relation;
- result.

Therefore differentiated attribution is not merely an external methodological rule.

It is supported by the structure of the Romans evidence itself.
