# 3. Repetition / Stable Evidence Map

## R01 — Повторяющееся прямое явление / обращение воскресшего Иисуса к Павлу

**Constituent units:**
- U07 — Деян. 9:1–9
- U15 — Деян. 18:9–11
- U16 — Деян. 22:6–10
- U17 — Деян. 22:17–21
- U18 — Деян. 23:11
- U19 — Деян. 26:12–18

**Общая реально разделяемая черта:** Павел получает post-ascension visionary/heavenly communication, в котором speaking/appearing figure локально идентифицируется как Иисус/Христос либо достаточно жёстко контролируется first-person content («о Мне»).

**Material differences:**
- U07/U16/U19 содержат explicit self-identification «Я Иисус».
- U15 использует title «Господь», принятую Stage 1 как достаточно идентифицируемую по local-book narrative context.
- U17/U18 используют discourse control («о Мне», «Я пошлю»), а не self-identification formula.
- функции речи различаются: command, encouragement, protection, sending, commissioning.

**Subject / Agency:** Christ / `Direct Action`.

**Strength / limits:** это strongest repeated structure Stage 2: несколько независимых narrative loci сходятся на непосредственной post-ascension речи/явлении Христа. Repetition claim касается формы явления/речи/направления, а не утверждения, что все subsequent ministry effects Павла являются direct Christ actions.

**Verification dependency:** none of the constituent units is currently retained for material verification.

# 4. Strong Singular Evidence

## S01 — U02 — Деян. 2:32–36
**Почему material:** explicit post-ascension action «излил» attributed to exalted Jesus after receiving promise from Father.  
**Почему не forced into repetition:** no other accepted unit presents the same Father → exalted Christ → Spirit-outpouring structure with equal explicitness.  
**Limitation:** mixed subject chain must remain visible.

## S02 — U09 — Деян. 9:32–35
**Почему material:** Peter explicitly states in present tense «Иисус Христос исцеляет тебя», followed by immediate result.  
**Почему singular/structurally distinct:** differs from Jesus-name mediation (U04/O01/U14): here Jesus Christ is explicitly named as healer in apostolic speech.  
**Limitation:** evidential level is speaker attribution within narrative, not narrator finite-subject clause.

## S03 — U05 — Деян. 5:30–32
**Почему material:** exalted Jesus as Leader/Savior is connected with giving repentance and forgiveness.  
**Почему singular/structurally distinct:** combines Father’s exaltation of Jesus with a continuing saving function; not identical to direct visionary speech or name-mediated action.  
**Limitation:** Role/action syntax remains less direct than U02/U07 etc.

## S04 — U06 — Деян. 7:55–60
**Почему material:** risen Jesus is seen in heavenly position and explicitly addressed in prayer.  
**Почему singular/structurally distinct:** evidence is Role/Relation + request, not narrated Christ action.  
**Limitation:** cannot be used as proof of narrated fulfillment.

## S05 — D20 — Деян. 20:22–32
**Почему material:** 20:28 may contain a materially important acquisition statement connected to “blood,” but accepted Stage 1 leaves text/referent unresolved.  
**Почему singular/structurally distinct:** textual/referential issue is unlike ordinary title ambiguity.  
**Limitation:** V18 blocks positive repetition use before verification.

# 5. Repeated Boundaries / Negative Evidence

## NB01 — Father-God attribution must remain explicit
Supported by R04. Repeatedly, Acts places decisive actions beside Christological material but names God as the actor. This is a stable anti-overattribution control.

## NB02 — Spirit attribution must remain explicit
Supported by R05. Repeated mission, guidance, filling, speaking and appointment actions are assigned to Spirit. No Stage 2 move may absorb them into Christ agency.

## NB03 — Human/Angel execution is not Christ direct action
Supported by R06. Apostles, believers and angels repeatedly perform actions in Christologically dense scenes; agency remains with those actors unless text provides an additional attribution.

## NB04 — Christ Role/Relation/content is not narrated Direct Action
Supported independently by U06, O02, D03, D10, D15, D17, D18, D21, D23, D26. Repetition exists in the boundary itself: Jesus may be seen, prayed to, named Judge, object of faith/preaching, or content of testimony without a narrated act.

## NB05 — Jesus-name mediation is distinct from Jesus as grammatical acting subject
Supported by R03 and contrasted with S02/U09. Name-related effectiveness is material but cannot be flattened into simple direct-action wording.

## NB06 — Unresolved «Господь» is a repeated attribution problem
Supported by R07. Repetition of the title cannot itself settle christological referent.

# 6. Non-merges / False Similarities

## NM01 — Direct Christ speech/appearance ≠ apostolic action in Jesus’ name
R01 units (U07/U15/U16/U17/U18/U19) contain Christ speaking/appearing. R03 units (U04/O01/U14) contain humans acting in relation to Jesus’ name. These must remain separate.

## NM02 — Christ Role ≠ Christ Action
U05, U06, O02, D10, D17 show continuing role/state/office or relation. These cannot be merged with R01 merely because Christ is active in the wider theology of the narrative.

## NM03 — Prayer to Christ ≠ narrated fulfillment by Christ
U06 presents explicit prayer addressed to Jesus but no narrated statement that Jesus performed the requested act. D09 similarly shows prayer before miracle without named addressee/actor.

## NM04 — God acting through humans ≠ Christ acting through humans
D14/D19 explicitly connect mission/miracle outcomes to God; U11 is “Lord” mediated through apostles but unresolved as Christ. Similar outward phenomena do not establish same subject.

## NM05 — Unresolved Lord ≠ explicit Jesus self-identification
R07 must not be merged with R01. U01/U03/U10/U11/U13/D12/O03 remain referent-dependent; U07/U16/U19 explicitly self-identify Jesus.

## NM06 — Repeated narrative retellings ≠ independent separate kinds of action
U07, U16 and U19 include related Saul/Paul encounter traditions. They legitimately support repeated textual witness to Christ appearance/speech, but Stage 2 must not treat narrative repetition of a related event as multiple unrelated historical action-types.

## NM07 — Christ-name mediation ≠ explicit Jesus-heals attribution
U09/S02 is structurally stronger/different than U04/O01/U14 because the speaker explicitly names Jesus Christ as healer rather than only invoking His name.

## NM08 — Spirit action ≠ Christ action even where Christ relation is nearby
U12 especially must remain mixed/unresolved: explicit Spirit guidance plus a later “Lord” inference cannot be harmonized into one Christ-action sequence before V13.
