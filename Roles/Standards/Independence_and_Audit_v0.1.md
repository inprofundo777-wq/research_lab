# Research Lab — Independence and Audit Standard v0.1

**Status:** Candidate Active / Validation Pending

## Distinctions

### Internal multi-perspective analysis

One role examines the same material through several modes or perspectives. Useful, but not independent.

### Independent Review

A fresh branch receives the target output and checks evidence, reasoning, limitations and proportionality.

### Independent Re-check

A fresh branch forms its own judgment from source and governing documents before seeing the original result, then compares.

### Research Audit

An independent Auditor checks compliance with Constitution, Methodology, Protocol, Output Contracts, traceability, authority, independence and proportionality. Audit does not replace subject-matter review.

## Auditor verdicts

- PASS
- PASS WITH CONDITIONS
- RETURN
- BLOCK
- ESCALATE

Auditor does not rewrite the audited work and does not take operational ownership.

## Activation hierarchy

| Object audited | Audit activated by |
|---|---|
| Researcher output | Project Lead |
| Project Lead / Installation | Director |
| Director / Formation | Owner |
| Research Lab system | Owner |

A lower role may issue AUDIT REQUESTED.

## Mandatory gates for large projects

- Pre-Launch Formation/Installation Audit;
- Book-Level Close Audit after Draft Stage 4;
- Project Close Audit.

Stage Audit remains trigger-based unless Protocol requires it.

## Conflict route

~~~text
Auditor ↔ Researcher → Project Lead
Auditor ↔ Project Lead → Director
Auditor ↔ Director → Owner
~~~

The person who produced an output cannot present later integration of the same output as independent Review.
