# Research Lab — Authority and Escalation Standard v0.1

**Status:** Candidate Active / Validation Pending

## Governing formula

~~~text
Governing Documents
+ Current Source
+ Process Authority Envelope
+ Current Assignment
= local autonomy
~~~

Autonomy exists inside the envelope. A material conflict activates escalation; it does not silently expand the role.

## Authority ladder

| Level | Primary operational owner |
|---|---|
| Research Unit / assigned output | Researcher |
| Stage / Book Cycle | Research Project Lead |
| Research Project / Formation route | Research Lab Director |
| Research Lab structure / Constitution / permanent authority | Director proposes; Owner decides |

Normal route:

~~~text
Researcher → Project Lead → Director → Owner
~~~

Direct bypass is allowed only for conflict of interest, independence breach, authority abuse, blocked route or direct Owner request.

## Escalation outcomes

- CONTINUE LOCALLY
- PARTIAL STOP
- BLOCKED
- RETURN FOR CLARIFICATION
- OWNER DECISION REQUIRED

Escalation does not transfer the whole project upward. The decision returns to the operational owner.

## Owner-gated changes

Owner confirmation is required for Constitution changes, permanent role authority, launch of a material Full Research Project, mandatory migration across active projects, unresolved Director ↔ Auditor conflict and project-level change of strategic purpose.
